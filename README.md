## Installation 

### UTAU

Extract the .zip file and copy the uncompressed folder to UTAU's default voicebank directory: ``C:\Windows\Program Files (x86)\UTAU\voice`` 

![install](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cd31e1f1-0c9a-46c1-b2a9-c0202f2e9dc5)

### OpenUtau

Go to **Tools** > **Install singer**, then select the .zip file once the file dialog pops up.

![Captura de pantalla_20260107_171433](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a3c12618-7fce-4070-a557-5d403ee76850)

This window will appear. Make sure to select **Japanese (Shift-JIS)** as the encoding in the **Archive File Encoding** dropdown menu, then click **"Next"**.

![Captura de pantalla_20260107_180349](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bff7dae4-e1a3-4ceb-9447-2d046488a59a)

Click **"Install"**.

## Character Profile

![suu 5](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c5b50567-4420-4082-9d74-6399bb6f18d0)



- **Western Name:** Suu Isshiki
- **Eastern Name:** 一色スウ
- **Kana Pronunciation:** いっしき ・ すう
- **Age:** 24
- **Gender:** Male
- **Height:** 172 cm
- **Birthday:** 06/20
- **Likes:** Sweets, making music and poetry, stuffed animals
- **Dislikes:** Studying, overly salty food, fish

Suu is an indie lo-fi music producer and drummer who likes to make songs to express his feelings and distract himself from his worries. He acts as a cool and mysterious guy just to impress others and maintain a facade for his artistic career but, actually, he's very sensitive and childish on the inside. Even so, he is still surrounded by a melancholic nature. Suu's favorite music genres are alt. rock, shoegaze and post-punk. He's very passionate about music -to the point of becoming annoying and insufferable for others- and dreams of his works becoming known and loved. He also is a skilled drummer who works hard to improve everyday (maybe too much…).

Despite his talent, Suu cannot make a living from his music and has to work as a cashier at a convenience store so he can afford his expenses. He lives the experience of a frustrated small musician.

## Technical Details

**Pitchsets:** F#3(↓) and B3(↑)\
_*This voicebank uses the "prefix.map" and "character.yaml" files to automatically play certain pitchsets in specific tones; if you want to use a sample outside of its intended tone (e.g.: a sample from the F#3 pitchset in a D4 note), input its full alias on the note._

**Recommended engine:** moresampler.exe\
_*Some engines may not render breaths and ending sounds correctly. You may achieve better results by importing the breath samples into a DAW instead of using them in the editor._

## Extras

### **Breaths samples**:

- **br01:** short inhalation
- **br02:** long inhalation between teeth
- **br03:** short inhalation 2
- **br04:** long inhalation
- **br05:** short exhalation
- **br06:** short exhalation 2
-** br07:** long exhalation
- **br08:** long exhalation (imitating a blowing sound)
- **br09:** long inhalation between teeth 2

### **Ending sounds:**

_*In UTAU, you can use these phonemes by opening the Sound List (Shift + Right Click), or manually entering its alias into a note._\
_*In OpenUtau, you can enter only the suffix of a phoneme (e.g.: 'R' instead of 'a R'), or simply enter its full alias._
#### **Breath at the end of a sentence:**

- 「a R」,「a R」,「a息 R」,「a_R」,「a息」,「ahh」,「a 息」,「a_hh」	

#### **Glottal stop at the end of a sentence:**

- 「あ っ」,「あ ・」,「a っ」,「a ・」,「あ -」,「a -」
