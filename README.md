## Character Profile

![suu 5](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c5b50567-4420-4082-9d74-6399bb6f18d0)


- **Western Name:** Suu Isshiki
- **Eastern Name:** 一色スウ
- **Kana Pronunciation:** いっしき ・ すう
- **Age:** 24
- **Gender:** Male
- **Height:** 172 cm
- **Birthday:** 06/20
- **Likes:** Sweets, making music and poetry, stuffed animals
- **Dislikes:** Studying, overly salty food, fish

Suu is an indie lo-fi music producer and drummer who likes to make songs to express his feelings and distract himself from his worries. He acts as a cool and mysterious guy just to impress others and maintain a facade for his artistic career but, actually, he's very sensitive and childish on the inside. Even so, he is still surrounded by a melancholic nature. Suu's favorite music genres are alt. rock, shoegaze and post-punk. He's very passionate about music -to the point of becoming annoying and insufferable for others- and dreams of his works becoming known and loved. He also is a skilled drummer who works hard to improve everyday (maybe too much…).

Despite his talent, Suu cannot make a living from his music and has to work as a cashier at a convenience store so he can afford his expenses. He lives the experience of a frustrated small musician.
